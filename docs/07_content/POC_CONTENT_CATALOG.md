# POC Content Catalog

## Population
12 named dwarves maximum; 4-player-adventurer party.

## Classes
Fighter, Rogue, Wizard, Cleric.

## Factions
Dwarven Hold, Cave Kin, Goblin Clans, Wanderers.

## Creatures
Cave Bat, Cave Wolf, Giant Beetle, Goblin Scout, Goblin Raider, Fungal Stalker, Stone Slime, Deep Spider, Ancient Construct, Cavern Wyrm.

## Mutations
Extra Arm, Night Vision, Regeneration, Stone Skin, Chitin Armor, Acid Spit, Telepathy, Photosynthesis.

## Tools/weapons
Iron Pickaxe, Stone Pickaxe, Iron Sword, Copper Spear, Wooden Shield, Short Bow, Torch, Rope.

## Buildings
Bed, Door, Stockpile Marker, Workshop, Forge, Campfire, Farm Plot, Barracks Fixture, Hospital Bed, Stairs.

## Dungeon
One three-to-five-level cavern/dungeon chain ending in an ancient sealed structure.
