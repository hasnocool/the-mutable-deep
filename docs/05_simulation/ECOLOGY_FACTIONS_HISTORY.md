# Ecology, Factions and History

## Ecology

Creatures have habitats, food preferences, reproduction/respawn rules, migration pressure and faction relationships. POC ecology remains intentionally small.

## Factions

POC factions: Dwarven Hold, Cave Kin, Goblin Clans, Wanderers. Each tracks disposition, territory, known leaders, resources, allies and enemies.

## Relationships

Important characters track friendship, trust, fear, loyalty and hostility toward other entities.

## History

Significant events become immutable history entries:

```text
EventId
Tick
Actors
Location
Cause
Outcome
Tags
NarrativeSummary
```

History can later feed rumors, quests, memorials, faction reactions and world generation.
