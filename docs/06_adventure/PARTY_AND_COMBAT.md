# Party and Combat

## Party

POC supports a four-character adventuring party drawn from the same world entities used by fortress mode.

## Control modes

- Click/select party members
- Group movement
- Individual movement
- Follow/hold-position commands
- Real-time with pause

## Combat loop

```text
Perceive → Initiative/priority → Choose action → Resolve → Apply effects → Re-evaluate
```

Actions include move, attack, defend, use item, ability, spell, interact, flee.

## Positioning

Terrain, doors, stairs, line of sight, elevation and hazards affect combat. Do not require a bespoke combat arena.

## Death and recovery

Death changes world state. Bodies, dropped equipment, witnesses, memories, faction reactions and history are eligible consequences.
