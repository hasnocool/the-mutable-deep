# Phase 02 — Fortress Simulation

## Objective
Turn the world into a living settlement.

## Deliverables
8–12 dwarves, jobs, pathfinding, mining, hauling, construction, workshops, stockpiles, beds, rooms, inventory and needs.

## Job priority
Player-set priorities influence job scoring. Citizens still decide among valid work based on skill, distance, needs and traits.

## Demo
Designate a mining area and workshop. Dwarves mine stone, haul it, build a room, craft a basic tool, eat, sleep and recover.

## Exit criteria
The fortress runs for an extended simulation interval without requiring manual micromanagement of each citizen.
