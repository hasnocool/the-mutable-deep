# Phase 01 — 3D World POC

## Objective
Prove the world representation, verticality and editable terrain.

## Deliverables
64×64×32 grid, chunk storage, basic meshing, orthographic camera, surface/mountain/underground generation, stairs, mining designation, pathfinding and simple material inspection.

## Demo
Player digs a room, cuts a tunnel, discovers copper, moves through stairs, and sees the same edited terrain from multiple camera angles.

## Exit criteria
Terrain changes survive save/load; NPC navigation recalculates after edits; average mesh rebuild remains interactive for the POC chunk size.
